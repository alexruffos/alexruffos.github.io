Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

YouTube Factory Archive is a personal-use application for backing up and restoring video production files through Google Drive using rclone.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to access and manage files in the owner's Google Drive.

Data Storage

Video production files are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. OAuth credentials are maintained locally by rclone.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising.

Revoking Access

The owner may revoke access at any time through Google Account security settings.
